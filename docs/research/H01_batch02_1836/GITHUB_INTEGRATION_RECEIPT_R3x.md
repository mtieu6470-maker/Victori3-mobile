## Research outcome

- Lye (1969, pp.96–97): 60k–80k is a composite estimate of Mzilikazi-led polity subjects, including Nguni/Sotho and military/subjugated/tributary/vassal categories; indigenous Sotho formed the greater part. It is not an ethnic Ndebele/Nguni core census.
- Lye's separate 1836 figure of 1k–5k refers to Nguni military force, plus subject people. The 10k–20k followers who crossed the Limpopo belong to a later withdrawal after 1836–37, not the 1 Jan 1836 total.
- Delius (2010), citing Rasmussen (1978, pp.23–26, 90–94), reports 300 as the plausible initial migrating group, ~20k as the most reliable end-1820s estimate, and some estimates 3–4x higher; he cautions that there is ample room for error. Warren et al. (2014) repeats the 300-to-20k trajectory with Sotho-Tswana incorporation.
- Rasmussen's cited pages have only been traced indirectly; direct page verification and the R3l 45k–85k interval method remain open.
- NDE 60k is retained only as a provisional approximate circa-1830s polity-subject candidate; 45k–85k remains an unverified range; NDE_MOS 210k remains broad geography, not proven direct rule. No numeric approval.
