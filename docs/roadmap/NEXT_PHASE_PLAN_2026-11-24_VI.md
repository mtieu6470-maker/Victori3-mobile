# Next phase plan (owner 2026-11-24: "still rudimentary; think about the direction")

Two parallel tracks: **depth** (Claude: mechanics, so that every play feels like Victoria 3) and **volume** (ChatGPT + Hydrogen: content and media in bulk, ingested by Claude's licence-checked tools).

## Phase A — core gameplay loop (each step ends with an APK for the owner to test)

1. **Diplomatic play** (spec input CG-53): staged crisis before war (demands, escalation, sway offers, back-down, AI resolve and confidence). Replaces instant war declarations.
2. **War: fronts, generals, battles** (CG-51): fronts on the map, commanders with traits, battle reports, naval invasions, recruitment and mobilisation panel.
3. **Treaties with articles and companies** (CG-54): trade, transit, base, investment, monopoly and guarantee articles with binding terms; companies with charters and monopolies.
4. **Politics depth:** parties and elections; law enactment panel showing support/opposition and the chance to advance or stall; political movements with agitators.

## Phase B — content delivery (the 4–5 GB target)

5. **Downloadable content packs:** a small APK plus packs installed offline after the user approves. Packs cover maps (high-resolution tiles), illustrations, portraits, flags, music and texts, with pack versioning, integrity hashes and storage checks.
6. **Media at scale** (ChatGPT + Hydrogen, licence-gated ingest):
   - illustrations for every event, building, good, technology and era (target thousands);
   - portraits for rulers, generals and politicians;
   - flags and coats of arms by period;
   - public-domain recordings of 19th–20th century music for themes per region and era, plus ambient sound;
   - high-resolution relief and terrain tiles.

## Phase C — flavour for every country

7. Event packs for the 8 major countries (MSG-126), then all playable countries. Each pack has V3-style journals: unification, reforms, revolutions, colonial arcs. Target 2,000+ events.

## Phase D — finish

8. Tutorial and tooltips everywhere, music and sound integration, map visuals, Android performance at 12h/s on real devices, save migration, and a release candidate.

**Rule:** every slice ships tests, an APK link for the owner, and updated board, handoff and parity records.
